About FeelCam

The future of emotional intelligence through facial expression analysis.

Our Mission

FeelCam was born from the idea that understanding human emotion is the key to better communication and technology. Using MediaPipe for high-fidelity landmark detection and a custom-trained deep learning model, we provide instantaneous insights into 7 core human emotions.

How it works

Our system captures facial landmarks in real-time, mapping 468 points on the human face to identify micro-expressions that even the human eye might miss. These patterns are then processed through our neural network to provide a probabilistic distribution of emotional states.
